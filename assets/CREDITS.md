# Credits

## Design

The "expedition atlas" design (map, field notes, stitched patches, compass screen) was made for this site. The playful, game-like approach to motion was inspired by <https://sepafshar.com/>, by the workshop author. No styles, fonts, or artwork are taken from it.

## Fonts

The site uses only fonts that are already on the visitor's device. Headings use the first available of Iowan Old Style, Palatino, Book Antiqua, or Georgia. No font files are downloaded.

## Art

- **Quest Bot** (`quest-bot.svg`), the map, the patches, and the line icons are original SVG drawings made for this site.
- The house drawing in `../bug-hunt/house.svg` and the placeholder art in `../template/images/` are also original.
- The site includes no historical photographs, logos, or game assets.
