Quest to Publish

A Playful, AI-Assisted Workshop on Building and Sharing Digital Humanities Projects

In three hours, participants with no coding experience use the free version of Claude or ChatGPT, together with GitHub and GitHub Pages, to publish a small website about their own research. The workshop is organized as a series of quests: teams earn collaborative badges, hunt for mistakes in AI-written content, and take on an accessibility challenge before sharing what they built.

  • 3 hours
  • No coding experience needed
  • Free tools
  • Team badges, no leaderboard

Field notes

The quest path

Six quests lead to a shared finale. When everyone on your team has finished one, press Complete to collect its coins and badge.

  1. Before you set off

    Pack your bag

    Bring these and you are ready for the trail.

    What you need

    • A laptop with an up-to-date web browser.
    • A free GitHub account.
    • A free Claude or ChatGPT account.
    • Two or three images that you are allowed to share.
    • One sentence about your research project.
    See the starter template
  2. Cleared

    Quest 0 20 min

    Briefing

    Goal: Meet your team and see where the quest leads.

    What you do

    • Tour the quest map and a finished example site.
    • Form small teams and share your research interests.
    • Agree on how to ask for and offer help.

    Team badge · Locked · 20 coins Trail Crew

  3. Cleared

    Quest 1 25 min

    Base Camp

    Goal: Get every tool ready before building.

    What you do

    • Sign in to GitHub and to the free version of Claude or ChatGPT.
    • Copy the starter template into your own repository.
    • Describe your project in one sentence and decide what not to share with an AI tool.

    Team badge · Locked · 20 coins Camp Builders

    Use the starter template
  4. Cleared

    Quest 2 40 min

    Build

    Goal: Turn your research into a first web page.

    What you do

    • Ask the AI assistant to help you edit the template.
    • Add a title, a description, images you have the right to use, and a note about yourself.
    • Read every suggested change and check its facts before you keep it.

    Team badge · Locked · 20 coins Story Builders

  5. Cleared

    Quest 3 25 min

    Go Live

    Goal: Publish your site at a public web address.

    What you do

    • Upload your files and turn on GitHub Pages.
    • Open your new link on a phone and on a laptop.
    • Troubleshoot with a teammate until every site loads.

    Team badge · Locked · 20 coins Beacon Lighters

  6. Cleared

    Quest 4 20 min

    AI Bug Hunt

    Goal: Learn to question what AI produces.

    What you do

    • Zap six planted errors on the practice page.
    • Compare findings and explain how you caught each one.
    • Run the same checks on your own site.

    Team badge · Locked · 20 coins Bug Spotters

    Play the Bug Hunt
  7. Cleared

    Quest 5 20 min

    Access for All

    Goal: Make your site usable by more people.

    What you do

    • Move through a site using only the keyboard.
    • Check alt text, heading order, and color contrast.
    • Swap sites with a teammate and fix at least one barrier.

    Team badge · Locked · 20 coins Open Door

  8. Cleared

    Finale 20 min

    Showcase and Debrief

    Goal: Celebrate the work and reflect on how it was made.

    What you do

    • Each team shows a live site and one thing it learned.
    • Discuss where AI helped, where it misled, and how you could tell.
    • Lay out your six badges and choose a next step for each project.

    30 coins No new badge here: the finale celebrates the six that your team collected together.

Trail rules

How the quest works

  • Earn badges together

    A badge belongs to the whole team and is earned when every member finishes the quest. Helping a teammate is part of winning.

  • Question the AI

    AI assistants draft and explain. Participants stay in charge: they read every change, check facts, and verify sources.

  • Publish for everyone

    Each project goes live on the web and then gets an accessibility pass, so more people can read and use it.
